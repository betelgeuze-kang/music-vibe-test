Time to focus

A note for this moment

A favorite song and a song that stays quietly beside your work are not always the same. This sequence begins with a small piano pattern and gradually adds more layers. Notice whether your attention changes as the music changes.

There is no promise of improved concentration. If lyrics or changing textures distract you, stay with the first track or switch the music off. The last selection is closer to a listening break at the end of a work session than a neutral background.

A way through the sequence

Use the recurring piano as a small point of reference, then follow the accumulating density in Experience. The linked performance is the 2013 London live version. Wait brings vocals and a larger dynamic range, so it may ask for more attention. It is placed last as a transition after one piece of work is finished.
